Common Questions

Everything you need to know about our Solo matches.

How does the Solo Reward System work?
Very simple! You pay a flat 150 PKR entry fee. In the match, every single kill you get earns you 50 PKR. So if you get 4 kills, you earn 200 PKR (50 PKR profit). Plus, top placements get additional prize pool shares!
How do I get the Room ID and Password?
Once you register for a match, the Room ID and Password will be visible on your dashboard and sent to your WhatsApp exactly 15 minutes before the match starts.
What if someone uses hacks?
We have zero tolerance for cheating. Moderators spectate every match. If someone is caught hacking, they are permanently banned, and their kills/points are invalidated. Affected players are compensated.
