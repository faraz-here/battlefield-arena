FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know before jumping into the arena.

How do I join a tournament?
Create your account, deposit funds via EasyPaisa/JazzCash, browse available tournaments, and click "Join Now". The room ID and password will be shared 15 minutes before the match starts via your dashboard and WhatsApp notification.
How do I receive my winnings?
Winnings are credited to your Arena Wallet automatically after match verification. Withdraw anytime to your EasyPaisa or JazzCash account — minimum withdrawal is ₨100, processed within minutes.
What if someone is cheating or hacking?
We have a zero-tolerance anti-cheat policy. All matches are reviewed through death cams and spectator reports. Cheaters are permanently banned and entry fees are refunded to fair players.
Can I get a refund if I can't play?
Yes! Request a refund anytime before the tournament starts and receive an instant full refund to your wallet. Once the match begins, refunds are not available.
How does the 1v1 wager system work?
Select your bet amount (minimum ₨50), get matched with a random opponent at the same bet level, play a TDM match, and the winner takes double the bet. Results are verified through match screenshots and game data.
Is buying UC from Battlefield Arena safe?
100% safe. We use official Midasbuy methods to top up UC directly to your PUBG account. Thousands of successful orders with zero bans. Delivery within 5-30 minutes guaranteed.
